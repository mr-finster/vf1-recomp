# The seed book

A YouTube comment asked: "How to know what to write in seed?" Here's every name that works, and what each one gives.

## How Seed works

A random fighter is made of three of the cast. Its head, body, arms and legs come mostly from those three (now and
then a part is anyone's, the cut characters and Dural too). Its moves are dealt from the same three, and its voice is
one of theirs. Its name is made from theirs, and you see it on the energy bar: JACPAIWOLF, SAJACWOLF, WOLFLAURAH.

Type that name as a **Seed** and you get that fighter again: the same build and the same moves, on any machine.
Only the colours change: they're new every game.

- **Offline:** Menu → Cheats → **Player 1 fighter** (or **Player 2 fighter**) → **Seed**, then type the name.
- **Online:** Online → Settings → **Your fighter** → **Seed**, and type the name in the **Seed** row under it.
  Your opponent sees it under your name on the VS screen.

A name that isn't in this book says "No fighter has the seed ...". The book is for the latest version: if an
update changes how fighters are made, some names may change too.
Every Random fighter you meet has one of these names, so if you liked one, write down its name from the energy bar.

What the columns mean: **Body** is the torso and hips, **Arms** the upper arms, forearms and hands, **Legs** the
thighs, shins and feet. A part can come from someone outside the three (Dural, Siba, Jeff, Takeru, the Koskol
brothers Rekka, Score and Aeon). **Moves** are dealt group by group from the three; the first is whose stance it has.
